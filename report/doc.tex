\section{Background}


\label{sec:introduction}
Housing instablity exists as a critical socioeconomic issue within Australian
society. Despite high levels of economic prosperity, escalating housing
pressures and persistent homelessness continue to affect Australians
\cite{absEstimating, aihw2023australia}, placing disproportionate 
burden on marginalised and lower-income groups, such as
young people, migrants and renters~\cite{daley2018housing}.

Over the past two decades, housing costs in Australia have increased
significantly relative to incomes, with home-ownership for all age
groups under 65 decreasing~\cite{daley2018housing}.
